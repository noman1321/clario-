Capabilities

Built for global collaboration

No more language barriers. Our AI handles translation, transcription and audio — all in real time.

Live Transcription

Your voice is captured and transcribed in real time using Gemini's multimodal AI — accurate, fast, and context-aware.

Instant Translation

Each participant sees captions in their own chosen language — simultaneously, with no delay between speakers.

Dubbed Audio

Hear the speaker's voice translated and spoken aloud in your language using Gemini's neural TTS engine.

Team Chat

Send messages during a call — no switching apps. Chat history stays in the session for all participants.

Admit Control

First participant becomes host. New joiners wait in the lobby until the host admits or denies them.

Zero Setup

Open a link, share a room code. Works in any modern browser — no extension, no install, no account required.
